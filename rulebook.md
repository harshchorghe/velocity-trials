# Velocity Trails — Official Rulebook

Welcome to **Velocity Trails**, the premier hybrid elimination tournament of TechChase 2K26. This rulebook outlines the mechanics, constraints, and elimination conditions for all three stages of the competition.

---

## 1. General Rules
1. **Authentication:** All players must authenticate at the main terminal to receive their official `AG_ID`. This ID tracks your Leaderboard Score across all levels.
2. **Scoring:** The ultimate victor is the player who accumulates the highest total Leaderboard Score across all three levels.
3. **Integrity:** Any attempt to bypass the physical or digital constraints of the game will result in immediate disqualification.

---

## 2. Level 1: Scavenger Hunt (Physical Arena)

**Format:** Individual | **Environment:** Real-world Arena | **Time Limit:** 3 Minutes

### Objectives
- Search the designated physical arena to find hidden materials and clues.
- Piece together the clues to deduce the Master Code.
- Enter the Master Code into the web terminal before the timer expires.

### Scoring & Penalties
- **Base Scoring:** Points are awarded based on completion speed.
- **Time Penalty:** If a player exceeds the 3-minute time limit, they will receive **negative points for every 15 seconds** they go over time.
- **Elimination:** There are no direct eliminations in Level 1. **All players** progress to Level 2, carrying their scores (including negative points) with them.

---

## 3. Level 2: Trial Run (3D Digital Realm)

**Format:** Multiplayer | **Environment:** Shared 3D World | **Time Limit:** 3 Minutes

### Objectives
- Enter the shared multiplayer digital environment.
- Locate and collect your **3 Velocity Crystals**.
- You may utilize a **Navigator** (a designated helper) to guide you toward the lost crystals.

### Hazards & Survival
- Players begin with **3 Lives**.
- The environment is filled with hazards (Robots, Lasers, Viruses). Each hazard hit costs 1 life.

### Eliminations & Penalties
A player is immediately eliminated from the entire tournament if they trigger any of the following conditions:
1. **Zero Health:** The player loses all 3 lives.
2. **The Deficit Rule:** If a player enters Level 2 with a negative score from Level 1 **AND** exceeds the Level 2 time limit by 1 minute, they are immediately eliminated.

Players who survive and successfully collect their crystals advance to the final level.

---

## 4. Level 3: Astra's Core (Boss Fight)

**Format:** Individual | **Environment:** 3D Arena | **Time Limit:** 4 Minutes

### Objectives
- The surviving players face off individually against the Corrupted Overlord.
- **The Boss:** The Overlord is significantly stronger in this phase and fights alongside a unique **Pet**.
- **Armament:** Prior to entering the arena, players must select one weapon class:
  - *Shadow Sword* (Melee / Speed)
  - *Gravity Blaster* (Ranged / Knockback)
  - *Elemental Spear* (Balanced / Freeze)

### Scoring & Victory
- Players have exactly 4 minutes to deal as much damage as possible to the boss.
- Points are strictly awarded based on the total HP damage dealt to the Corrupted Overlord.
- **The Victor:** Once all players have completed their boss run, the system aggregates the scores from Levels 1, 2, and 3. The agent with the highest overall Leaderboard Score is declared the winner and earns the right to Reboot Humanity.
